BuddyTalk+ Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

BuddyTalk+ ("the app", "we", "us") is an English speaking practice app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, name, and a verification code (OTP) sent to your email to sign you in. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and verified email from Google.
  • Profile and learning data: your English level, goals, interests, lesson progress, scores, coins and activity inside the app.
  • Voice and microphone: the app asks for microphone permission so you can practise speaking and take part in voice calls. Speech recognition is performed by your device's speech recognizer. Voice during live calls is transmitted to the other participant in real time.
  • Chat and call data: messages you send to other users or mentors, and basic call information (who called whom, when, and duration).
  • Online status: if you turn on the "Online" option, other users can see that you are available, along with your first name, initials and level. We do not collect or share your GPS location.
  • Device and technical data: push notification token, app version and error logs, used to deliver notifications and fix problems.

2. Fingerprint / biometric login

If you enable fingerprint login, verification is done entirely by your phone's operating system. Your fingerprint data never reaches the app or our servers.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and send sign-in codes.
  • To provide lessons, games, speaking practice, chat and live calls.
  • To match you with other learners or mentors.
  • To send notifications you have allowed.
  • To keep the community safe, handle reports and block abusive users.
  • To improve and fix the app.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With other users, limited to what is needed for the feature (for example your first name, initials and level when you are online, or your messages in a chat).
  • With service providers that help us run the app, such as hosting, email delivery and push notifications.
  • When required by law or to protect the safety of users.

5. Data storage and security

Data is stored on secure servers and transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time by emailing us at the address below. We will delete it within a reasonable time, except where we must keep some records by law or for safety reasons.

7. Children

BuddyTalk+ is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices

  • You can turn off microphone or notification permission in your phone settings.
  • You can turn off your Online status inside the app.
  • You can request access, correction or deletion of your data by contacting us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept them.
