BuddyTalk+ – Delete Your Account

App: BuddyTalk+ · Developer: BuddyTalk+ team

You can ask us to delete your BuddyTalk+ account and the data linked to it at any time. This page explains how.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to buddytalk999@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my BuddyTalk+ account.
  3. Write the email address you used to sign up for BuddyTalk+. Send the email from that same address so we can confirm the account is yours.

We will confirm by reply and delete your account within 30 days of confirming the request.

Data that is deleted

  • Your account details (email address, name or username, password)
  • Your profile and app progress (levels, XP, streaks, coins, game and task progress)
  • Your chat messages and practice session data stored on our servers

Data that may be kept

  • Basic security and abuse-prevention logs, kept for a short period (up to 90 days) and then removed
  • Records we are required to keep by law, if any

Request deletion of some data only

If you want some of your data deleted without deleting your account, email us at the same address and tell us which data you want removed.

Contact

Questions? Write to buddytalk999@gmail.com.
